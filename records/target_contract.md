# Research checkpoint LEM-20261102-A

The requested goal is a high-level computational complex-analysis working paper
on clustered-root polynomial lemniscates, now explicitly in the official
Preprints.org LaTeX format. This is a research phase, not authorization to post.

## Fixed mathematical scope

Let P_e(a+e w)/(e^m q(a)) = F_e(w) = Q(w)G_e(w), with Q squarefree,
q(a) nonzero, e positive, and the external zeros outside a fixed local disk.
Main critical points are simple and have nonzero critical values. The
connectivity convention is the partition of labelled roots in CLOSED sublevels;
numerical comparisons use regular levels, away from exact saddle values.

The target is a quantitatively useful theorem and reproducible computations,
not a guarantee of originality or publication. A complete mathematical proof,
validated finite certificates, a scoped literature comparison, and an honest
account of computational limits constitute a useful first checkpoint.

## Routes executed in this phase

R1: retain complex inverse moments of the external roots before taking absolute
values; derive logarithmic and derivative remainder bounds, then transfer them
to labelled minimax merger levels. Falsifier: wrong sign, missing barrier,
failure under constant factors, or counterexample to a partition sandwich.

R2: use the quadratic stationary value at any computational center, with a
Rouche disk and explicit third-derivative remainder. Falsifier: a polynomial
violating an enclosure despite satisfying its strict localization hypothesis.

R3: test the numerical hook against exact quadratic geometry, including grid
translations, orientations and both digital adjacency conventions. Compare
distance-only and moment-aware guarantees on equal-degree external fields.
This is not an adaptive-algorithm superiority benchmark.
