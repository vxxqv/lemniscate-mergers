## Current conclusion

No theorem is labelled globally novel. The normalization, moment expansion,
minimax comparison, Taylor completion and basic saddle width are elementary or
classical mechanisms. The exact quantitative statements have been derived here,
but derivation is not evidence of historical originality. The potential paper
contribution is their cluster-specific combination and verified usefulness.

Before a novelty claim, read the two closest full texts and compare constants,
hypotheses, output guarantees, computational costs and certification range.
Search citation descendants and older root-neighborhood/pseudozero terminology.
The new exact-quadratic digital-connectivity theorem also needs comparison
against sampling and digital topology literature. Its proof is internal; its
historical originality is not established.
